Safety by design

Useful guidance should know when to stop.

FaultCite is designed around evidence, authorization and escalation. It does not authorize bypassing guards, interlocks, safety circuits or employer lockout/tagout procedures.

  • Safety and energy-state checks stay visible during repair work.
  • Unsupported conclusions should be escalated instead of presented as certainty.
  • Restart approval remains an authorized human decision.
  • Uploaded manuals and evidence remain company-scoped.

FAQ

Questions before a pilot

Is FaultCite a CMMS?

FaultCite is focused on CNC troubleshooting evidence and repair knowledge. It is not positioned as a replacement for every planning, inventory and work-order function of a full CMMS.

Does FaultCite replace OEM manuals or qualified technicians?

No. FaultCite supports trained maintenance personnel and preserves applicable source material and evidence. OEM procedures, employer safety rules and qualified judgment remain authoritative.

Can different companies see each other's records?

The application is designed around tenant-scoped company workspaces and role-controlled access. Cross-company isolation is part of the release acceptance process.

Where do users sign in?

Authorized company users sign in at app.faultcite.com using the work email invited by their company.
