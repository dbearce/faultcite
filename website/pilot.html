What good looks like

A pilot should answer whether FaultCite belongs in your maintenance workflow.

The goal is not a staged demo. It is to see whether technicians can use the system during real breakdown work while managers retain clear evidence and control.

  • Technicians can identify the correct machine and start or resume the right case.
  • Evidence, manuals and diagnostic results remain attached after reload.
  • Manager-only approvals remain protected by role checks.
  • Company records stay isolated during two-company acceptance testing.
  • The team can export and review a complete repair history.
