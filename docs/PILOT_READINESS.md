# FaultCite outside-company pilot readiness

FaultCite remains a controlled pilot until every required gate below has an
owner, date, and attached evidence. A checked box without evidence is not a
release decision.

## Product acceptance

- [ ] Create a new pilot organization; never reuse FaultCite Internal Pilot.
- [ ] Record the customer safety contact and FaultCite support contact.
- [ ] Invite one technician and a different manager/approver.
- [ ] Register one machine from its nameplate.
- [ ] Upload a customer-licensed PDF and confirm document rights.
- [ ] Approve the manual metadata and at least one exact-page source mapped to
      the registered machine.
- [ ] Complete the two-account acceptance script below without sharing a login.
- [ ] Confirm all case timeline events display with actor name and email.
- [ ] Confirm the closed case is read-only and remains visible after sign-out.

## Two-account acceptance script

1. Technician signs in, selects the pilot organization, and opens a machine-down
   case.
2. Technician verifies machine identity, records the symptom and alarm, and
   opens the applicable approved exact-page source.
3. Technician records one structured observation and attaches appropriate
   evidence, or records a specific evidence exception when file evidence is not
   applicable.
4. Technician requests manager review and confirms the review-requested time,
   due time, queue age, and notification.
5. A different manager signs in and reviews the observation, exact source, and
   evidence before confirming the supported cause.
6. Technician records the repair summary, parts, labor, verification readings,
   safety-device check, and test cycles, then requests closeout.
7. Manager reviews the closeout, approves restart, and closes the case.
8. Both users verify the complete immutable timeline and export record.
9. Attempt prohibited transitions: self-approval, edits after close, cross-company
   access, duplicate submission, and restart without required verification. Each
   must be rejected without changing the audit record.

## Device and accessibility acceptance

- [ ] Current iPhone Safari portrait and landscape.
- [ ] Current Android Chrome portrait and landscape.
- [ ] Desktop Chrome, Edge, and Safari where available.
- [ ] Keyboard-only completion with visible focus and no focus trap.
- [ ] Screen-reader labels and status announcements on all required fields,
      errors, notifications, and state transitions.
- [ ] Browser zoom at 200% with no lost controls or horizontal reading flow.
- [ ] Shop-floor glare review: supporting text remains readable and statuses do
      not depend on color alone.
- [ ] All primary touch targets are at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels.

## Operational acceptance

- [ ] Production health check is monitored continuously.
- [ ] FaultCite has a named incident owner and an alert destination.
- [ ] Support mailbox is staffed for the hours promised to pilot customers.
- [ ] Database and uploaded-file backups are encrypted and inventoried.
- [ ] Restore the latest database and files into an isolated environment; record
      timestamps, checksums, row/object counts, discrepancies, and approver.
- [ ] Record approved recovery time and recovery point objectives.
- [ ] Record the escalation path for safety-critical reports and privacy events.

## Commercial and professional approval

- [ ] Attorney-approved pilot agreement, privacy notice, terms, data retention,
      deletion/legal-hold process, manual-license language, and liability terms.
- [ ] Industrial safety adviser approval of product claims and pilot controls.
- [ ] Insurance broker confirmation of appropriate cyber, E&O/professional, and
      general liability coverage.
- [ ] Written pilot scope: one machine group, named users, 60–90 days, success
      measures, weekly review, stop conditions, and exit/data-return process.

## Pilot measures

Track only values supported by saved records:

- completed source-backed cases;
- evidence-complete percentage;
- median and 90th percentile manager review time;
- active downtime and temporary-repair backlog;
- repeated technician use;
- support requests and time spent supporting the customer;
- qualitative technician and manager feedback.

FaultCite now records structured product feedback, support requests, optional
case references, priority, and contact requests from the Help & Safety page.
Managers can use the operations report to review median and 90th-percentile
manager review time, repeat technician use, and open feedback counts. A blank
measure means the pilot has not produced a sufficient saved sample; it is not a
zero. Review urgent feedback through the staffed support process because the
in-app form is not an emergency alert channel.

### Weekly pilot review evidence

- [ ] Export or record the median and 90th-percentile manager review time with
      its sample size.
- [ ] Review repeat technician use and distinguish adoption from duplicate or
      training cases.
- [ ] Triage every open support request and urgent submission; record owner,
      disposition, and response time outside FaultCite until a staffed support
      workflow is formally approved.
- [ ] Summarize technician and manager feedback without including unnecessary
      personal, medical, or confidential production information.
- [ ] Record whether pilot stop conditions were triggered.

Do not promise downtime or financial savings until a customer-approved baseline
and measurement method exist.
